## What this shows

- Pairing removes the variation the two models share (hard passages are hard for both). On the same blocks the unpaired intervals overlap while the paired interval of the difference does not include 0.
- Intervals from 8 KiB blocks are wider than from 256-byte blocks: neighbouring windows are correlated, so the per-window intervals elsewhere in this project are somewhat optimistic about absolute bpb.

## Caveats

- Post-hoc: registered after the unpaired verdict was known. The pre-registered verdict stays 'not supported'; this answers a better-posed question, it does not overturn the first one.
- Blocks inside a play are still correlated (dialogue, recurring names); 8 KiB blocks reduce that, they do not remove it. Three plays give a sign per play, not an interval.
- One seed per model: this says nothing about run-to-run variance, which a paired test on fixed checkpoints cannot see.
